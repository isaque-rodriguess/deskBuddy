Your desktop, but cuter.

An animated pet mascot that floats over your desktop, reacts with animations, and wanders while idle.

Download Latest View on GitHub
Rust Tauri 2 SvelteKit

Features

👻

Transparent Overlay

Borderless window with 60fps sprite animation. Your pet lives on your desktop, not in a window.

🚶

Roam AI

The pet pauses, walks, and mirrors direction naturally while idle. It has a mind of its own.

🖱️

Drag & Drop

Pick up your pet and drop it anywhere. Drop above ground and watch it fall with gravity.

🐾

PetDex Integration

Browse 3000+ community pets, download your favorites, and switch anytime.

⚙️

Settings

Size slider, roam toggle, on/off toggle. Customize your pet from the system tray.

🚀

Autostart

Launch on boot (Windows & Linux). Your pet is always there when you need it.
